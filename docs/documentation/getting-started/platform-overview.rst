=================
Platform Overview
=================

Kavanah Analytics pulls data from your connected platforms, stores it
in Elasticsearch, and makes it available through three workspaces:

- **Dashboards** — Pre-built dashboards for each platform plus
  Digital Impact Reports, automatically filtered to show only the data
  your account is authorised to see.
- **Dashboard Builder** — A drag-and-drop interface for creating
  custom charts and composing your own dashboards. Used by admins to
  produce the dashboards everyone else views.
- **Data Explorer** — A natural-language query assistant powered by
  Claude. Ask questions in plain English and get charts, tables, or
  the underlying ES query back.

Data is imported on demand or on a schedule. You control the date
range, which platforms to pull, and whether to refresh cached data.
