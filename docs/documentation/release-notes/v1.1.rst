v1.1 — October 2026
===================

Release date: October 2026

A feature release on top of :doc:`v1.0-beta`. The standard upgrade
procedure applies: back up, pull, restart the workers. No migration step
is needed; the one new set of organization fields is added automatically
the first time it is used.

----

What's New
----------

**Sharing with the Global.Church shared map (opt-in, off by default)**

An organization can choose to contribute its country-by-month reach to
the `Global.Church <https://global.church>`_ shared map. A release holds
four whole-number counts per team × country × month — exposed,
interactions, touchpoints and active — plus a short manifest. Teams are
identified only by an opaque identifier; nothing below country grain and
nothing naming a person ever leaves, and the file format cannot carry
anything finer.

Sharing is off for every organization until its workspace owner turns
it on, either at signup or from the organization's new sharing page,
after reading what leaves, what stays, and the data-sharing agreement.
Publishing is a deliberate action from the new **Publish to Global.Church**
page: choose teams and countries, choose whether to be named, accept
the agreement, publish. Kavanah records the acceptance and the receipt
only after Global.Church accepts the file. Turning sharing off stops
every future release and keeps nothing hidden about what was already
sent.

The whole feature sits behind a platform switch that is off on a fresh
installation and on Kavanah's hosted service until the first live
publish has been reviewed.

See :doc:`../guide-management-console/global-church-sharing`.

----

Fixes
-----

**Team form: organization pre-fill is scoped by workspace.** When an
organization in one workspace had the same name as an organization in
another, the team Add form could offer both and then refuse the save.
The pre-fill now only ever offers organizations the current user can
see.
